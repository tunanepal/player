Android

Install on Android

Takes about ten seconds. No Play Store needed.

  1. 1Open tunanepal.com in Chrome.
  2. 2Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right corner.
  3. 3Tap Add to Home screen, then Install.
iPhone

Install on iPhone

iPhone installs from Safari — this will not work in Chrome.

  1. 1Open tunanepal.com in Safari.
  2. 2Tap the Share button at the bottom — the square with an arrow.
  3. 3Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Questions

Is it safe to install?

Yes. It installs from your browser, not from a file, so nothing is downloaded to your phone.

Does it take much space?

Under 1 MB. It also opens without internet, though you need a connection to join a match.

How do updates work?

Automatically. Every time you open the app you have the newest version — nothing to reinstall.
